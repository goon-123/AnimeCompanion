# Schedule and Home weekly widget — 0.38.0 (97)

1. Open Schedule → Weekly Schedule. Switch between **Continuous** and **Single day**. Choose a different weekday in each mode; its highlight and content should follow your selection. Change weeks, use the date picker, and tap Today.
2. In Single day, switch **List / Grid**. iPhone shows three poster columns at standard text sizes; iPad adapts to available width. Check upcoming vs. already-aired groups, full list titles, episode numbers, Sub/Dub labels, local release times, and countdowns. Empty dates should remain selectable.
3. Leave and reopen the app. The saved Continuous / Single day and List / Grid choices should remain. Changing All / Sub / Dub or Everyone / My list should filter either mode without changing the selected date.
4. In Continuous, compare short and long titles across several days. Posters should share the same leading edge, with consistent padding and metadata. Today's whole section should retain its accent background and rail. Scroll to the last item and footnote to check bottom-bar clearance.
5. On Home, check **Airing this week**: seven columns, Sunday–Saturday; only your library anime; distinct-series count when several share a date; empty placeholders otherwise. Today stays accented, with a plus when empty. Changes to your library should update the widget. Pull Home down to refresh release data.
6. Tap an occupied day, another weekday, and today's empty plus. Each should open Weekly Schedule with that exact date selected, respecting your saved viewing mode and filters. Repeat with the Schedule tab hidden, then on iPad in portrait, landscape, and your preferred sidebar/split layout. Home customization can hide or reorder the new widget.

Unknown and delayed releases remain labeled. A release without a confirmed date cannot populate a weekday. Device and visual checks are intentionally left to the user; the longer simulator suites remain opt-in.
